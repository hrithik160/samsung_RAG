# Galaxy S24 reference fixture

## Display

The Galaxy S24 features a Dynamic AMOLED 2X display with a 120Hz refresh rate.

## Camera

The Galaxy S24 camera includes a 50MP main sensor, a 12MP ultrawide, and a 10MP telephoto with 3x optical zoom.

## Battery

The Galaxy S24 battery capacity is 4000mAh. It supports 25W fast charging and 15W wireless charging.

## S Pen

The base Galaxy S24 does not support the S Pen. The Galaxy S24 Ultra includes a built-in S Pen.
