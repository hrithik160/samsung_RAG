# Workshop venue reference fixture

## Pune venues

Venue A in Pune accommodates up to 30 people for workshops. Venue B accommodates up to 50 attendees.

## Cancellation

Venue A allows cancellation without charge up to 48 hours before the workshop. Later cancellation is charged one day.

## Catering

Venue A offers on-site catering. External caterers are allowed with prior approval.
